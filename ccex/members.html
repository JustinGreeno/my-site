The People Behind CCEx

Meet Our Volunteers

The Cumberland County Exhibition is made possible by dedicated volunteers who give their time and energy to keep this tradition alive. Our board members represent diverse backgrounds in agriculture, business, and community service, united by their love for CCEx.

Click on any member to learn more about them!

Board of Directors

Holly Martin
Director

My name is Holly Martin. I grew up in Amherst. I have spent 26 years working for Province of NS, in Child Welfare.

I feel I can help the CCEX BOD, as I have many years of Board and Committee Experience, most recently, 3 Terms on BOD for NSGEU, representing 39,000 members. Last year I joined CCEX BOD. As a Board, we have been able to improve and grow the exhibition, constantly looking for new opportunities, funding, and activities.

Last year, we were able to partially re-open the dining room, providing some home-cooked meals. My goal for this year is to offer 3 days with home-cooked meals.

I also committed to assist in organizing a Saturday CCEX Parade.

Please consider becoming a volunteer!

Josh Greeno
Chair Person

Josh lives in Linden, NS on his Beef & Dairy Farm – P&S Farms. Josh spends his spring, summer and fall on the farm doing custom cropping (hay, silage, wheat, oats and straw) along with regular upkeep of farm equipment and daily farm management.

In the winters Josh works hard keeping roads cleared as a snow plow operator in Pugwash, NS. This is Josh's first year as CCEX Chair Person, but he has been either a volunteer or director of the exhibition for the past 5 years.

Committees: Exhibition Maintenance • Youth Dairy Show

Kylynne Sheffield
Director

Kylynne lives in Linden, NS with her partner Josh Greeno – CCEX Chair Person. Kylynne works in Pugwash, NS at Sunset as a Residential Counsellor.

When she's not working at Sunset she's farm or pet sitting in the area, or helping out on Josh's farm. Kylynne is also on the board of directors for the Maritime Shorthorn Association as well as a leader in the Linden 4-H Club.

Committees: Sponsorship • Vendor Hall • Show'N Shine • Youth Dairy Show
