About the Game

A deeply story-driven narrative team-based RPG. Six unlikely young adults are drawn together and given the opportunity to be heroes, whether they asked for it or not.

Inspired by Final Fantasy, Zelda, and Stardew Valley. Built entirely solo, all artwork, programming, narrative, and music direction. Every pixel hand-drawn in Aseprite.

"In the darkest of times, even the smallest light can bloom into something extraordinary."

Ellie, Darkness Blooms

The Story

The main story follows overarching themes that challenge what you think about heroes and villains. Expect a journey built on consequence, identity, and the gray spaces between good and evil.

"It's like everyone tells a story about themselves inside their own head. Always. All the time. That story makes you what you are. We build ourselves out of that story."

Patrick Rothfuss
