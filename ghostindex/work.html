Darkness Blooms

All Devlogs →

A story-driven narrative team-based RPG. Six unlikely young adults drawn together and given the chance to be heroes, whether they asked for it or not. Built solo: all pixel art, programming, music direction, and narrative design. Inspired by Final Fantasy, Zelda, and Stardew Valley.

Characters
Ellie, Nixxy, Billy, Asher, Player1, KK

Six original characters: a herbalist, a thief, a scholar, a dog, you, and a martial artist. Each hand-crafted in Aseprite.

Technology
Unity + C# + Aseprite

Built in Unity with custom C# systems. All art created in Aseprite, original sprites, animations, and tile sets.
